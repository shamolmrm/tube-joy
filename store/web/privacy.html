Privacy Policy

Last updated 28 October 2026

🎮 Who we are

Tube Joy: Water Sort Puzzle ("the game") is made by BahaLabs. This policy explains what information the game uses and why.

📱 What stays on your phone

Your levels, coins, themes and settings are saved only on your device. There is no account and no sign-in, and we do not run servers that collect your game data.

📢 Ads (Google AdMob)

The game is free and shows ads from Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect your device's advertising ID, approximate location (from your IP address), app interactions and crash diagnostics. Google uses this information under its own policy: How Google uses information from apps that use its services. Ads are limited to family-friendly content.

✅ Your choices

In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland we ask for your consent before personalised ads, and you can change your choice at any time in the game under Settings → Privacy options. On any Android phone you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Settings → Google → Ads.

🔒 Sharing and selling

We do not sell your personal information. Apart from Google's ad services described above, the game does not share data with anyone.

👶 Children

Tube Joy is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will help.

🗑️ Deleting your data

Uninstalling the game removes all game data from your phone. Ad-related data is handled by Google; you can delete your advertising ID as described above.

🔄 Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
